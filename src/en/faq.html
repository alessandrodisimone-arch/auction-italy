Can a foreigner buy a property at an Italian auction?

Yes. You need an Italian tax code (codice fiscale) and a valid ID. The tax code is easy to obtain and we can help you get it.

Are you a real estate agency?

No. We do not match buyers and sellers on the open market. We provide legal and technical assistance to people who want to buy at public auctions, mainly those of the Court of Fermo.

How is this different from buying through an agency?

An agency starts from your wishes and searches for homes. Here you look at the properties the Court is selling and decide whether they suit you. The big advantage is the price: auction properties often sell 25% to 50% below market value, depending on how many previous auctions went unsold.

Who will assist me?

A lawyer registered with the Bar Association of Fermo. The register is public and anyone can check it.

Do I need a lawyer to take part?

The law does not require it, but it is strongly advised, especially if you do not speak Italian. The procedure is technical, all documents are in Italian with no official translation, and Italian property law is hard to navigate without experience.

Where do auction properties come from?

Most come from foreclosures after unpaid mortgages, loans or other debts. The Court sells them publicly to repay the creditors.

Who runs the auction?

A professional appointed by the Court, usually a lawyer or an accountant (commercialista). A judge supervises the whole procedure and signs the transfer decree after the sale.

Where can I see the current auctions of the Court of Fermo?

All public auctions of the Court of Fermo are published on astalegale.net.

How does the process work in short?
  1. Visit the property (booked through the court custodian).
  2. Pay a deposit, refunded if you do not win.
  3. Bid online on the Ministry of Justice platform.
  4. If you win, pay the balance within the Court's deadline.

Can I visit the property before the auction?

Yes. Visits are arranged through the court-appointed custodian (custode giudiziario). We can book them and come with you.

Could the previous owner still be living there?

Often, yes. The debtor may stay until the sale is completed, then the Court proceeds with the vacating of the property. We follow this step for you.

How can I be sure everything is legitimate?

Check the official listing on astalegale.net or at the Court's offices. The deposit, the balance and the taxes are always paid directly into the Court's bank account shown in the official notice. We never handle our clients' money.
