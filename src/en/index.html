How we help

From the first listing to the keys in your hand

Italian court auctions are open to everyone, but the documents are only in Italian and the procedure is technical. We are not an estate agency: we are on your side, from start to finish.

  1. Choose 1–3 properties

    Browse the Court's current auctions and tell us which ones interest you. We help you understand what you are looking at.

  2. Visit them

    We book viewings with the court-appointed custodian and can accompany you in person.

  3. Get a clear report

    We review the official appraisal, legal status, occupancy and real costs, and explain everything in plain English.

  4. Bid online

    We prepare your offer and deposit and support you during the online auction on the Ministry of Justice platform.

  5. Complete the purchase

    After you win, we follow the payment of the balance, the transfer decree and the vacating of the property.

  6. Receive the keys

    We stay with you until the Court hands over the keys of your new home.

Why Fermo

We live and work here. We know the towns, the neighbourhoods and how the Court of Fermo handles its cases.
