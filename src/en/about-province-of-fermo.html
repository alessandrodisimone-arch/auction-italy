Province of Fermo

Italy's best-kept secret on the Adriatic

Between the sea and the Sibillini Mountains, the province of Fermo offers sandy beaches, medieval hill towns and quiet countryside, at property prices among the lowest in Central Italy.

One province, every landscape

In the morning you can sail on the Adriatic, in the afternoon walk the trails of the Sibillini Mountains. In between: rolling hills, vineyards and villages that have hardly changed in centuries.

Because the area is still little known to international tourism, prices remain accessible. Some towns, like Servigliano, already have a lively community of foreign residents, especially from the UK.

Places to know

Where to look for your home

  • FermoThe medieval capital: cathedral, Teatro dell'Aquila, Piazza del Popolo and the Roman cisterns.
  • Porto San GiorgioSeaside town with sandy beaches, a marina and a lively promenade.
  • Torre di PalmeA tiny hilltop village with stunning views over the Adriatic.
  • Sibillini MountainsNational park with trails, peaks and mountain villages like Amandola and Montefortino.
  • Sant'Elpidio a MareHilltop fortress town with festivals and countryside views to the sea.
  • PetritoliListed among the most beautiful villages in Italy.
  • Santa Vittoria in MatenanoMedieval village with palaces, churches and frescoes.
  • PedasoSmall beach town with a fishing tradition.
  • ServiglianoQuiet countryside town, popular with English-speaking residents.
  • The coastLido di Fermo, Marina Palmense and Porto Sant'Elpidio: relaxed beaches for the summer.
